# Gameplay and controls

Two people take turns on one board. On the default 10×10 board, White starts with Amazons on a4, j4, d1, and g1. Black starts on d10, g10, a7, and j7. The board keeps the same orientation between turns.

1. Select one of your Amazons. Circles mark legal destinations.
2. Slide it horizontally, vertically, or diagonally through empty squares. The board previews the relocation and marks legal shots with diamonds.
3. Choose an empty square along a clear horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line from the new square. The arrow permanently blocks its landing square. You can aim through or land on the square you just left.

An Amazon or arrow stops movement and shots beyond its square. The player whose turn begins with no legal move loses. The winning side is displayed above the rematch button.

Click or tap squares to play. To use the keyboard, Tab to the board, move with the arrow keys, and select with Enter or Space. Escape backs up one stage. Pointer users can choose **Cancel selection** or **Change destination** before shooting. Shooting commits the entire turn and adds it to move history.

Reloading restores the last completed turn in the same browser. New game asks before replacing a match in progress. If browser storage is unavailable, the current tab can still finish the match.

Choose **New game** to select a board size. On 8×8, White starts on a2, e1, and h3. Black starts on h7, d8, and a6. Cancel preserves the current match. Start game replaces it, and Play again uses the size just played.
